00:01 — Features

Everything the player does

Twelve things VidMax handles well, in place of the fifty it doesn't try to.

Local playback

Video and music from your device, no extra setup.

Network streaming

Connect over SMB, FTP or WebDAV and browse remote folders directly.

Stream link

Paste a URL — HLS, DASH, SmoothStreaming or plain HTTP.

Online music

Discovery feed and streaming, no account required.

Dual engine

Media3 as primary, MPV underneath for wider codec support.

28 themes

Fully offline, including a true AMOLED black.

Subtitles

SRT support with adjustable style and position.

Volume boost

Push playback up to 200% past device max.

Gestures

Swipe for volume and brightness, pinch to zoom.

Playlists

For video and music, plus favorites on both.

Aa

Font changer

Built-in set, or import your own TTF / OTF.

Auto updates

Checks GitHub releases and notifies you in-app.

00:05 — Privacy

What VidMax doesn't do

No advertising SDK, no ad tracking, no mandatory account, no subscription for core playback. Your media library stays on your device — network features only reach the servers or links you configure yourself.
